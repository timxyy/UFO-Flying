One of my earliest properly completed and working projects. A game where you fly a UFO through ten levels with lava obstacles and a final boss fight.
The code is absolutely awful, but it's endearing to see where I started.
Shockingly, I created relatively realistic physics with acceleration and velocity from a stroke of complete luck considering I had no physics education at the time.
Unfortunately, I had no such insights on the programming side, and the code is completely hard coded from beginning to end with absolute nonsense happening in terms of scope and design.
Still, it functions and it is uplifted by the delightful original art and music by my friends Alan Liao and Matthew Tsai.
Truly proof that tenacity and persistence can overcome even the greatest issues in skill.
